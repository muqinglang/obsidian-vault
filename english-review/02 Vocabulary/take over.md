# take over

`take over` 可以指接管任务或公司，也可以指某件事逐渐占据生活。

> She asked another teammate to take over the unfinished task.

> I don't want short videos to take over my free time.

不要与 `take ownership of` 混淆：后者强调主动承担责任。
