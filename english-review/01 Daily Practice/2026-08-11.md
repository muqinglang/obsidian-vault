# 2026-08-11

## 今日主题

向英语老师或朋友介绍一个社交媒体构想：创建具有传统中式审美的 AI 男性角色，制作轻松、带克制幽默的美食内容。

## 核心表达

> I'm thinking about creating an AI character for social media.

> He would have a relaxed vibe with subtle, quiet humor.

> I'd rather use an AI character than appear on camera myself.

> I think this character could appeal to a lot of women.

> I want the humor to feel natural rather than exaggerated.

关联：[[I'm thinking about creating]]、[[would rather A than B]]、[[appeal to someone]]、[[a relaxed vibe]]。

## 本次纠正

### would rather

- ❌ `I'd rather use an AI character instead of appearing my face.`
- ✅ `I'd rather use an AI character than appear on camera myself.`
- ✅ `I'd rather use an AI character instead of showing my face.`
- 关联：[[would rather A than B]]

### 完整名词短语

- ❌ `He has relaxed with a subtle quiet humor.`
- ✅ `He has a relaxed vibe with subtle, quiet humor.`
- ✅ `He has a relaxed personality and a subtle sense of humor.`

- ❌ `The vibe is relax.`
- ✅ `The vibe is relaxed.`
- 关联：[[形容词与名词形式]]

### 自然表达“吸引”

- ❌ `Most girls or women can like this character.`
- ✅ `This character could appeal to a lot of women.`
- ✅ `A lot of female viewers might find the character appealing.`

### 双主语

- ❌ `A lot of women, they may like this character.`
- ✅ `A lot of women may like this character.`
- 关联：[[双主语]]

## 发音

- `subtle` /ˈsʌtəl/：`b` 不发音，见 [[subtle 的 b 不发音]]。
- `appealing` /əˈpiːlɪŋ/：重音在 `PEAL`。
- `character` /ˈkærəktər/：重音在第一音节。
- `traditional` /trəˈdɪʃənəl/：重音在 `DI`。

## 同日复习补充

- [[one size doesn't fit all]]、[[nudge]]、[[willpower]]、[[valid]]、[[take over]]。
