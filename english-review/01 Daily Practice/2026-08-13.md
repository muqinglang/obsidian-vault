## 1. 取舍与兼顾

### 主动回忆

1. `I am weighing a higher salary against more free time.`
2. `I am trying to balance work and family life.`
3. `I am considering changing jobs after the project ends.`
4. `Please weigh the package before you mail it.`

### 核心区别

- weigh A against B：比较两边利弊，准备取舍。
- balance A and B：同时兼顾两边。
- `consider both A and B`：把两个因素都纳入考虑。
- consider + doing：考虑做某事。

## 2. 今日 AI 英语：Study Mode

### 核心观点

学习型 AI 应帮助学习者逐步思考，而不只是给出最终答案。

### 表达

- `work through a problem`：一步步处理问题
- `step-by-step`：逐步地
- `instead of just ...`：而不是只……
- `common misconceptions`：常见误解
- `think in loops with AI`：和 AI 反复“提问—尝试—反馈—修改”

### 跟读

> Please explain the grammar step-by-step instead of just giving me the answer.

> I think in loops with AI when I practise speaking.
