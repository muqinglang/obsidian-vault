## 1. AI、人类价值与学习意义

### 整理后的观点

> Creativity is one of the qualities that makes humans unique.

> AI relies on data provided by humans, so it probably won't replace every part of human creativity.

> Humans build on ideas from previous generations. We create things not just for survival, but also for meaning and fulfillment.

> I want to experience new things and get new perspectives.

关联：build on ideas、not just for survival、make a living。

### 纠正

- `Every data is come from human provide.` → `Data comes from humans.` / `Data is provided by humans.`
- `AI ... instead human.` → `AI probably won't replace humans.`
- `People should learning.` → `People should learn.`
- `for survive` → `for survival`

## 2. 兴趣运动

> I've never tried rock climbing, but it looks really cool.

> It doesn't seem as scary as bungee jumping, and it still builds your upper body strength.

### 纠正

- `I've never been tried / I've never try ...` → `I've never tried ...`
- `as scary like` → `as scary as`
- `it can builds` → `it can build`
- `it still build` → `it still builds`
- `up body` → `upper body`

关联：I've never tried、as adjective as、rock climbing。

## 3. 外卖未收到

### 真实场景自然版本

> I ordered some takeout through an app, and it says the order has been delivered, but I haven't received it yet.

> I need to call the delivery driver and ask where my order is.

> The driver marked the order as delivered because the app has a delivery time limit. If drivers are late, they might get a warning or be penalized.

### 重点纠正

- `I didn't found it.` → `I didn't find it.` / `I couldn't find it.` → didn't + 动词原形
- `I need call ...` → `I need to call ...` → need to + 动词原形
- `they might be get a warning` → `they might get a warning` / `they might be warned` → 情态动词 + 动词原形
- `delivery limited time` → `delivery time limit`

关联：mark something as delivered、I was wondering if、penalized。

## 4. 职业取舍：weigh / balance / consider

> I'm weighing a higher salary against more free time.

> I'm trying to balance work and family life.

> I'm considering both the salary and the working hours.

> I'm considering quitting my job.

### 重点纠正

- `I am balance ...` → `I am balancing ...`
- `consider A against B` → `consider both A and B` 或 `weigh A against B`
- `I'm considering quit job.` → `I'm considering quitting my job.`

关联：weigh A against B、balance A and B、consider + doing、quit my job。
