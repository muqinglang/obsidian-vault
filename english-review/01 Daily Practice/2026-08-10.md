## 1. 台风与居家

### 自然版本

> Even though it's Monday, I'm staying at home because a typhoon is coming. It's raining heavily and very windy.

> The typhoon brought heavy rain and strong winds.

### 本次纠正

- ❌ `Even though it's Monday, but ...`
- ✅ `Even though it's Monday, ...`
- 关联：even though 不与 but 连用

- ❌ `I still at home.`
- ✅ `I'm still at home.`
- 关联：缺少 be

- ❌ `It's heavy rainy / raining heavy.`
- ✅ `It's raining heavily.` / `There is heavy rain.`

## 2. 工作与 AI 服务构想

### 整理后的表达

> I worked on fixing some bugs and my English learning plan. Then I watched about an hour and a half of a podcast on YouTube. That's the longest video I've watched in a while.

> I think I could offer AI services, such as deploying simple AI tools or automations for clients, depending on their needs.

> Some people want to know which Instagram posts work best, so I can use AI to analyze their posts and give suggestions. I can also draft a simple script for their next video.

### 重点

- offer AI services
- depending on their needs
- 双主语：`Some people, they ...` → `Some people ...`
- `want know` → `want to know`

## 3. 情绪与坚持

### 可复用表达

> I don't feel like talking to people right now.

> I'm feeling anxious and kind of restless—mostly about money.

> I'm feeling on edge. Money is stressing me out.

> I feel reluctant to give up.

> I got short-tempered with my husband tonight.

词汇：reluctant、restless vs on edge、short-tempered。

## 4. 航班延误微课

### 场景句

> My flight has been delayed because of bad weather. I'm trying to find out whether I should wait for this flight or switch to another one.

> I'm worried I might miss my connecting flight.

> Are there any other flights I could take instead?

> I was wondering if you could help me rebook my connecting flight.

### 纠正

- `My flight delayed.` → `My flight has been delayed.`（缺少 be / 被动语态）
- `Do you know when will it leave?` → `Do you know when it will leave?`（间接问句语序）
- `I'm very hurry.` → `I'm in a hurry.`

## 5. 听力与发音

- `It's hard to think` 在连读中曾被听成近似 `taught to`；这是听辨现象，不是语法错误。
- 练习优先捕捉 `not / didn't / isn't`，见 抓住否定词的听力策略。
- `reluctant`：re-**LUC**-tant，结尾轻收 `/nt/`，见 reluctant 发音。
- 复习 was about to 连读。
- 当天复习还包括 `depend on`、`stay in touch with` 和 `keep someone grounded`。
