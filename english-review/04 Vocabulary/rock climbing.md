# rock climbing

> I've never tried rock climbing, but it looks really cool.

> It builds your upper body strength.

## 发音

`climbing` 中的 `b` 不发音：/ˈklaɪmɪŋ/。

搭配：`try rock climbing`，不说 `try to rock climbing`。

## 语义关系

- 经验表达：[[I've never tried]]
