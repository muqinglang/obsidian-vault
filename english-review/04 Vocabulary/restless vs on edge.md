# restless vs on edge

## restless

坐立不安、静不下来，可能来自无聊、焦虑或精力无处释放。

> I'm feeling restless, mostly because I'm worried about money.

## on edge

紧张、易受惊或容易烦躁，像神经绷紧。

> I've been on edge all day.

## 自然组合

> I'm feeling restless and on edge. Money is stressing me out.
