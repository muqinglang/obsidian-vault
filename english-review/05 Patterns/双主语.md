# 双主语

普通英语陈述句不要同时保留名词主语和重复代词。

- ❌ `Some people, they want to know which posts work best.`
- ✅ `Some people want to know which posts work best.`

- ❌ `A lot of women, they may like this character.`
- ✅ `A lot of women may like this character.`

## 口诀

`名词主语` 和 `they/he/she` 二选一。
