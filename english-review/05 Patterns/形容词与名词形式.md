# 形容词与名词形式

## 本周例子

- ❌ `The vibe is relax.`
- ✅ `The vibe is relaxed.`

- ❌ `He has relaxed vibe.`
- ✅ `He has a relaxed vibe.`

- ❌ `I'm anxiety.`
- ✅ `I'm anxious.`

- ❌ `It's not very scare.`
- ✅ `It's not very scary.`

## 提醒

- 人的状态：`anxious / scared / relaxed`
- 事物造成的感受：`scary / relaxing`
- 形容词修饰单数可数名词时通常需要冠词：`a relaxed vibe`
