# 情态动词 + 动词原形

`might / can / could / should / will` 后接动词原形。

- ❌ `They might be get a warning.`
- ✅ `They might get a warning.`
- ✅ `They might be warned.`
- ✅ `They might be penalized.`

区别：`might get` 是主动结构；`might be warned/penalized` 是被动结构。
