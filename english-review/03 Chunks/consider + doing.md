# consider + doing

`consider` 后接动作时用动名词。

> I'm considering quitting my job.

> We're considering moving closer to the office.

- ❌ `consider quit`
- ❌ `consider to quit`
- ✅ `consider quitting`

谈“辞掉自己的工作”要说 `quit my job`，见 [[quit my job]]。
