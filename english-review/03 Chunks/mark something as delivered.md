# mark something as delivered

> The driver marked the order as delivered.

> Did you mark it as delivered before you arrived?

`delivered` 是“已经送达”，不是“正在派送”。

在外卖场景中，`driver` 最口语；`courier` 稍正式；`delivery person` 友好但较少用。
