# insist on doing vs persist in doing

## insist on doing

态度坚定，坚持要求、主张或采用某种做法。

> She insisted on testing the feature before release.

## persist in doing

即使遇到困难、阻力或失败，仍继续做。

> I persisted in speaking English even when I made mistakes.

## 记忆

- **insist on**：我要求这件事这样做。
- **persist in**：这件事很难，但我继续做。

不要说 `persist on studying` 或 `persist to do`。
