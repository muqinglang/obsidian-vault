# balance A and B

重点是让两方面同时保持在可行状态，不一定二选一。

> I'm trying to balance work and family life.

> We have to balance speed and quality.

进行时使用 `be + balancing`：

- ❌ `I am balance work and family life.`
- ✅ `I am balancing work and family life.`
