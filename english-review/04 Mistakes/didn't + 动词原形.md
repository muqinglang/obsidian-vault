# didn't + 动词原形

过去时已经由 `didn't` 承担，后面的实义动词用原形。

- ❌ `I didn't found it.`
- ✅ `I didn't find it.`
- ✅ `I couldn't find it.`

同理：`didn't go / didn't receive / didn't know`。
