# need to + 动词原形

表达“需要做某事”：`need to + 动词原形`。

- ❌ `I need call the delivery driver.`
- ✅ `I need to call the delivery driver.`

同一原则：`want to report`，不说 `want to reported`。

> [!note]
> 这里的 `to` 是不定式标记。介词 `to` 可接动名词，例如 `look forward to meeting you`。
