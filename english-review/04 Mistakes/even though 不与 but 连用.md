# even though 不与 but 连用

英语中 `even though` 已经表达让步，主句前不再加 `but`。

- ❌ `Even though it's Monday, but I'm still at home.`
- ✅ `Even though it's Monday, I'm still at home.`

也可改成：`It's Monday, but I'm still at home.`
