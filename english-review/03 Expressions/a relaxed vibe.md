# a relaxed vibe

> He has a relaxed vibe with subtle, quiet humor.

> The videos have a relaxed style rather than an exaggerated one.

`relaxed` 是形容词，需搭配名词 `vibe / personality / style`；或说 `The vibe is relaxed.`

## 发音

`subtle` /ˈsʌtəl/ 中的 `b` 不发音。分层练习：

`subtle → subtle humor → a relaxed vibe with subtle humor`
