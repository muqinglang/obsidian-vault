# consider + doing

`consider` 后接动作时用动名词。

> I'm considering quitting my job.

> We're considering moving closer to the office.

- ❌ `consider quit`
- ❌ `consider to quit`
- ✅ `consider quitting`

谈“辞掉自己的工作”时，单数 `job` 前需要限定词：

- ❌ `I'm considering quit job.`
- ✅ `I'm considering quitting my job.`
- 也可以说 `quit your job / quit his job / quit her job`。
