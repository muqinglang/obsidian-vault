# weigh A against B

把两边放在“天平”上比较，通常是为了作出选择。

> I'm weighing a higher salary against more free time.

> We need to weigh speed against accuracy.

不要说 `consider A against B` 或 `weigh A with B`。

## 区别

- [[weigh A against B]]：比较利弊，准备取舍。
- [[balance A and B]]：努力同时兼顾。
- `consider both A and B`：把两者都纳入考虑。

## 语义关系

- 易混表达：[[balance A and B]]
- 发音对比：[[weigh vs weight 发音]]
