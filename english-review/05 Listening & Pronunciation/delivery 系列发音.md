# delivery 系列发音

- `delivered` /dɪˈlɪvərd/：重音在 **LIV**。
- `delivery` /dɪˈlɪvəri/：重音同样在 **LIV**。
- `received` /rɪˈsiːvd/：重音在 **CEIVED**。
- `penalized`：重音在第一音节 **PE**。

练习：

> The driver marked the order as delivered.

> I haven't received it yet.

> The driver might be penalized for being late.
