# weigh vs weight 发音

- `weigh` /weɪ/：动词，权衡或称重；`gh` 不发音。
- `weight` /weɪt/：名词，重量；结尾有清楚的 `/t/`。

> We need to weigh the pros and cons.

> My suitcase weighs eighteen kilos.

> I'm trying to maintain a healthy weight.
