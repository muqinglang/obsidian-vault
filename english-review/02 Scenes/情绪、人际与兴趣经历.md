# 情绪、人际与兴趣经历

> [!goal] 沟通目标
> 描述当下情绪、人际反应和从未尝试过的活动，不把“人的感受”和“事物的特点”混在一起。

## 先说：不要看下面

先说最近的一种情绪，再说一次人际反应，最后介绍一个想尝试但还没尝试的活动。

## 自然表达

> I've been feeling restless and a little on edge, mostly because money has been stressing me out.
>
> I felt reluctant to give up, but I also got short-tempered with my husband. Talking things through helps keep me grounded.
>
> I've never tried rock climbing. It doesn't seem as scary as bungee jumping, and it looks like a good way to build upper-body strength.

## 情绪词汇

- [[reluctant]]
- [[restless vs on edge]]
- [[short-tempered]]
- [[valid]]

## 经历表达

- [[I've never tried]]
- [[rock climbing]]
- [[as adjective as]]
- [[insist on doing vs persist in doing]]

## 典型纠错

- [[even though 不与 but 连用]]：`Even though I'm anxious, I still want to try.`

## 听音

- [[was about to 连读]]

## 复现任务

- [ ] 用 `restless` 和 `on edge` 描述两个不同状态。
- [ ] 用 `I've never tried...` 介绍一项真实活动。
- [ ] 区分 `scary`（事物令人害怕）和 `scared`（人感到害怕）。
