# 外卖未收到

> [!goal] 沟通目标
> 说明“App 显示已送达，但我没有收到”，确认外卖员把订单放在哪里，并礼貌询问是否提前点了送达。

## 先说：不要看下面

用 45–60 秒回答：订单发生了什么？你检查过哪里？你希望外卖员做什么？

## 自然表达

> Hi, I'm calling about my food delivery. The app says the order has been delivered, but I haven't received it yet.
>
> I just checked downstairs, but I couldn't find it. Could you tell me exactly where you put it?
>
> I was wondering if you might have marked it as delivered a little early. Are you still on your way?

## 核心表达

- [[mark something as delivered]]
- [[delivery time limit]]
- [[I was wondering if]]
- [[penalized]]

## 典型纠错

- [[didn't + 动词原形]]：`I didn't found it.` → `I didn't find it.`
- [[need to + 动词原形]]：`I need call the driver.` → `I need to call the driver.`
- [[情态动词 + 动词原形]]：`They might be get a warning.` → `They might get a warning.`

## 复现任务

- [ ] 不看笔记完成一次 45 秒投诉。
- [ ] 必须说出 `has been delivered`、`haven't received it yet` 和 `where you put it`。
