# 航班延误与改签

> [!goal] 沟通目标
> 询问预计起飞时间、替代航班和转机风险，并礼貌请求改签。

## 先说：不要看下面

假设航班因天气延误，你担心错过转机。先独立说 45 秒，再看自然版本。

## 自然表达

> My flight has been delayed because of bad weather. I'm trying to find out whether I should wait for this flight or switch to another one.
>
> Do you know when it's expected to depart? I'm concerned that I might miss my connecting flight.
>
> I was wondering if you could help me rebook my connecting flight.

## 核心表达

- `be expected to depart`
- `switch to another flight`
- `miss my connecting flight`
- [[I was wondering if]]

## 典型纠错

- [[缺少 be]]：`My flight delayed.` → `My flight has been delayed.`
- [[间接问句语序]]：`Do you know when will it leave?` → `Do you know when it will leave?`

## 沟通修复

- [[What does X mean]]：询问 `rebook` 的意思。
- [[repeat again 冗余]]：没有听清时请对方重说。

## 复现任务

- [ ] 用三句话说明延误、担忧和请求。
- [ ] 把 `when will it leave` 改成正确的间接问句。
- [ ] 分别用普通请求和 `I was wondering if...` 请求改签。
