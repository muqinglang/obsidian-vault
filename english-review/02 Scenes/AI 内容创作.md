# AI 内容创作

> [!goal] 沟通目标
> 清楚介绍一个 AI 角色或 AI 服务：它是什么、面向谁、有什么风格，以及为什么这样设计。

## 先说：不要看下面

用 60 秒介绍你的 AI 男性角色，包括外貌、气质、内容形式、目标观众和你不亲自出镜的原因。

## 自然表达

> I'm thinking about creating an AI character for social media. He would be a handsome man with a traditional Chinese look.
>
> He'd have a relaxed vibe with subtle, quiet humor. I don't want him to be overly funny or exaggerated.
>
> I'd rather use an AI character than appear on camera myself. I think the character could appeal to women who like traditional Chinese aesthetics.

## 核心表达

- [[I'm thinking about creating]]
- [[would rather A than B]]
- [[a relaxed vibe]]
- [[appeal to someone]]

## AI 服务扩展

- [[offer AI services]]
- [[depending on their needs]]

## 典型纠错

- [[双主语]]：`A lot of women, they...` → `A lot of women...`
- [[形容词与名词形式]]：`The vibe is relax.` → `The vibe is relaxed.`

## 复现任务

- [ ] 先用 30 秒介绍角色，再用 30 秒说明它能提供什么价值。
- [ ] 必须使用 `would rather A than B` 和 `appeal to`。
- [ ] 朗读 `a relaxed vibe with subtle humor`，不要读出 `subtle` 中的 `b`。
