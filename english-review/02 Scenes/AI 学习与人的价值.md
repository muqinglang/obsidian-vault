# AI 学习与人的价值

> [!goal] 沟通目标
> 表达 AI 时代为什么仍要学习，以及 AI 应该怎样帮助人思考，而不只是替人完成答案。

## 先说：不要看下面

用 60–90 秒回答：如果 AI 能完成大部分工作，人为什么还要学习？

## 自然表达

> Creativity is one of the qualities that makes humans unique. Humans build on ideas from previous generations, and we learn not just for survival, but also for meaning and fulfillment.
>
> AI can take over some routine work, but one size doesn't fit all in learning. A good study tool should help us work through a problem step by step instead of just giving us the final answer.
>
> I have been trying to think in loops with AI: I ask a question, make an attempt, get feedback, and revise my answer.

## 核心表达

- [[build on ideas]]
- [[not just for survival]]
- [[make a living]]
- [[take over]]
- [[one size doesn't fit all]]
- [[instead of + doing]]
- [[have been trying to]]

## 典型纠错

- [[情态动词 + 动词原形]]：`People should learning.` → `People should learn.`
- `for survive` → `for survival`
- `AI instead humans` → `AI replaces humans` / `AI takes over some tasks`

## 学习沟通扩展

- [[wrap up vs sum up]]：区分“结束学习活动”和“概括所学内容”。

## 复现任务

- [ ] 先给观点，再给一个个人学习例子。
- [ ] 必须使用 `not just for... but also for...`。
- [ ] 用 `instead of + doing` 说明你希望 AI 怎样教学。
